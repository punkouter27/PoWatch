Alternative overview · diagram-design style

PoWatch, drawn in an editorial diagram style

The same system as the other pages, drawn by hand as four simple pictures: what runs where, how a frame becomes a story, how the code is layered, and where each kind of data may live.

These diagrams follow cathrynlavery/diagram-design (MIT): inline SVG on a 4px grid, hairline strokes, one accent for the headline path, orthogonal connectors with 8px elbows, uppercase arrow labels of 14 characters or fewer on paper masks, at most 9 nodes per diagram, and the legend as a strip at the bottom. Its light and dark palettes are applied through CSS variables. On narrow screens each diagram scrolls inside its frame.

1 · What runs where

Your device does the watching, the server does the counting, and outside services sign you in and (optionally) polish the words.

  1. SenseWorkers turn frames and sound into boxes and short text.
  2. QueueThe app packs 10-second ticks into the IndexedDB outbox.
  3. UploadEach batch is posted with a batchKey.
  4. Claim onceIngestService merges totals only on the first claim, then pushes statsChanged to the owner's open tabs.
  5. TellRecaps read the totals, and an optional model rewords them.
